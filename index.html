Data Sources & Provenance (5 Uploaded Files)

  • World Bank DataBank (.xls): Series [NE.TRD.GNFS.ZS] (Trade % of GDP); [TX.VAL.TECH.MF.ZS] (High-tech exports); [NY.GDP.MKTP.KD.ZG] (GDP growth).
  • IMF World Economic Outlook (.xlsx): Current Account Balance (% of GDP) representing the net trade margin.
  • World Trade Organization (.xlsx): Merchandise exports by product group (Gross export volumes).
  • UNCTADstat Data Center (.csv): Historical FDI inward and outward flows.
  • World Investment Report - Fact Sheet (.pdf): Baseline FDI stock and inward capital verification.

Note: GSO / Macro Estimates used to model FIE Domestic Value Added (DVA) structurally at ~25% due to high import reliance (assembly function). Domestic DVA modeled at ~65%.
